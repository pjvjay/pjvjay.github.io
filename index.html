Research

📄 LREC 2016 · Portorož, Slovenia · May 2016
Edison: Feature Extraction for NLP, Simplified
M. Sammons, C. Christodoulopoulos, P. Kordjamshidi, D. Khashabi, V. Srikumar, P. Vijayakumar, M. Bokhari, X. Wu & D. Roth  ·  University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

Edison is a feature extraction library for NLP that simplifies the process of defining and generating features for structured prediction tasks. It provides a unified interface over Illinois NLP tools and supports a wide range of linguistic annotation types — enabling rapid prototyping of NLP pipelines with minimal boilerplate. Published at the 10th International Conference on Language Resources and Evaluation.
